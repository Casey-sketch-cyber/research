## Plan drawings

- `home_D1_observed` adapts normalized geometry from Standfest et al., [Swiss Dwellings, version 3.0.0](https://zenodo.org/records/7788422), licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The project rendered the plan as a schematic, using the 29.97 m² D1 showcase.
- `home_D3_generated` and `plan_S{0,1,2}_{night,day,dinner}` use the project-generated 18.00 m² D3 shell. The nine layouts depict the normal-day H3 couple example from the saved [validated showcase layouts](../../../results/expA_f1/analysis/layouts/showcase/). Hatching indicates a stowed item. The green outlines in Figure 1 v2 mark the deployed and stowed bodies of the same validated wall bed in the Night and Workday panels.
- The [plan generator](../../../scripts/12_make_figure1_plans.py) checks the saved sources and regenerates all 11 SVG and transparent 600 dpi PNG pairs.

## Export formats

Figure 1 is 174 × 110 mm. It includes a vector PDF and a 600 dpi PNG. The graphical abstract is 5.5 in square. It includes a vector PDF and a 1,650 × 1,650 px, 300 dpi PNG. The [Cell Press graphical abstract guidelines](https://crosstalk.cell.com/hubfs/Files/GA_guide.pdf) list TIFF, PDF and JPG as preferred submission formats.
