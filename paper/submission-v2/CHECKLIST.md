# iScience Submission Package v2

## Final-format files

The upload directory contains the XeTeX-compiled main manuscript PDF, its TeX/BibTeX sources, standalone figures, graphical abstract, supplemental PDF and spreadsheet, and Word attachments. The review directory includes a XeTeX-compiled reading PDF with Figures 1–6 appended, plus the Word-based reading copy. Each affiliation, the lead-contact footnote and correspondence occupy separate lines on the title page.

- The main source uses the [official Cell Press LaTeX template v1.10](https://www.cell.com/pb-assets/journals/platform/authour-resources/cell-press-latex-template-1747231785383.zip), released 13 May 2025. Author and affiliation blocks use the template's `authblk` commands. The package includes the publisher's BibTeX style and support file.
- The publisher's [iScience Final File Requirements](https://www.cell.com/pb-assets/journals/EM/MasterFFCs/ISCIFFC.pdf) prefer editable Word and also accept LaTeX with a compiled PDF and source/support files. The manuscript follows the listed section order, moves the Key Resources Table to its own attachment and keeps main figures separate.
- Main citations use consecutive superscript numbering. Thirty approved bibliography entries are in first-citation order. The TeX log had no unresolved citations or missing glyphs.
- Acknowledgments include the Shenzhen Polytechnic University Postdoctoral Fund (Grant No. 6026331014K).
- Main figures are 174 mm wide, with single-page PDF and 300 dpi LZW TIFF options. The refreshed plots use approximately 6.5–7 pt labels. Figure 6 contains A–F. Former panel G is Figure S5, with the reviewer’s reader-facing kit labels.
- GraphicalAbstract.tif is RGB, LZW compressed, 1,200 × 1,200 pixels at 300 dpi, as specified by the iScience final-file checklist. The 4-inch PDF and the revised editable source use 16 pt title, 14 pt column headings and 12 pt body text, with a larger numeric callout.
- Supplemental figures are S1–S7 in citation order. Table S4 is an editable spreadsheet. The supplemental PDF excludes page numbers and includes its own bracketed reference.
- Highlights.docx contains four bullets of 65–80 characters. KeyResourcesTable.docx and CoverLetter.docx are separate. The blank official interests form is supplied for author completion.
- Research values are taken from the reviewed manuscript and committed figure inputs. The analysis results were left intact.

See [Author Actions and Scope](ISSUES.md) for author-supplied items and remaining checks. The form is the [publisher original](https://els-jbs-prod-cdn.jbs.elsevierhealth.com/pb-assets/shared/forms/di_form.pdf).
